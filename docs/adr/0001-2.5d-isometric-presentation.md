# ADR 0001 — 2.5D Isometric Presentation

- **Status:** Accepted product direction; camera/input details require vertical-slice validation.
- **Date:** 2026-10-19

## Context

The target is a 2.5D presentation inspired by _Battle of the Immortals_: 3D characters and environments composed for a fixed or semi-fixed isometric camera. _Diablo_ and _Torchlight_ are additional readability/composition references.

## Decision

Shattered Firmament will use Godot 4.x and render 3D characters and environments from a fixed or semi-fixed isometric perspective. Exact projection, camera pitch/yaw, movement scheme, rotation, zoom limits, controller support and platform compromises are validated in a vertical slice.

## Consequences

- Map blockout, silhouettes, VFX, ground targeting and occlusion must be evaluated from the intended camera.
- Combat telegraphs and entities must remain readable with multiple players and effects on screen.
- Web/mobile performance and Godot exports require explicit testing.
- Server simulation remains independent of client camera/rendering.
- Reference games inspire direction only; all assets and implementation must be original or properly licensed.

## Vertical-slice acceptance tests

1. Navigation is clear without depth/occlusion confusion.
2. Target selection and telegraphs remain readable.
3. At least three characters and one boss remain distinguishable in combat.
4. Safe-zone boundaries and collision are legible.
5. Web and native minimum-device performance budgets are declared before closed testing.
