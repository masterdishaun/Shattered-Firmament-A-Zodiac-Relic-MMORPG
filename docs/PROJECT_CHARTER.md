# Project Charter

**Product:** Shattered Firmament: A Zodiac Relic MMORPG  
**Status:** Approved product baseline; implementation proceeds through verification gates.  
**Repository:** This GitHub repository is the exclusive source of truth.

## Vision

Create a long-lived cross-platform MMORPG combining eastern martial-arts cultivation, zodiac mythology, celestial relic hunting, faction politics and large shared-world encounters. Reward mastery and community without pay-to-win mechanics, punitive attendance, destructive upgrades or gear-loss penalties.

## Reference frame

- _Battle of the Immortals_: 2.5D isometric camera/composition, Zodiac fantasy and classic MMO inspiration.
- _Jade Dynasty_: cultivation atmosphere, eastern-fantasy factions and spiritual progression.
- _Diablo_ / _Torchlight_: readable isometric action composition and environmental silhouettes.

These are inspirations only. All art, maps, characters, scripts, music, effects, UI and assets must be original or properly licensed.

## Product pillars

1. A world with a mystery: the Celestial Sundering happened for reasons hidden by all three factions.
2. Faction identity: three starting regions, narratives, mentors, questlines and early dungeons.
3. Meaningful progression: level 1–100, five cultivation realms, gear tiers and distinct build choices.
4. Shared adventure: open-world regions, solo story instances, party dungeons, raids and scheduled world bosses.
5. Chosen conflict: always-on designated faction PvP, contested objectives and protected peace areas.
6. A living economy: professions, crafting, global marketplace and secure direct trade.
7. Player trust: no paid-exclusive competitive power, no destructive upgrade failure, transparent odds and generous catch-up.
8. Operational longevity: measurable service health, low-population maintenance mode, backups and clear shutdown/data policies.

## Platform and perspective

Godot 4.x; 2.5D isometric presentation with 3D characters and environments under a fixed or semi-fixed camera. One client codebase targets web and native desktop/mobile, subject to export/performance validation. Real-time gameplay is server-authoritative.

## Non-goals for launch

- No unverified MMO-scale population/performance claims.
- No blockchain or real-money trading as a core economy requirement.
- No paid-exclusive stats, combat pets, combat skills or power progression.
- No unrestricted PvP everywhere.
- No unlimited uncapped combat-stat treadmill.
- No copying proprietary assets, Cube engine, code, exact UI or quest text from reference games.

## Decision governance

Canonical changes are documented in ADRs. Balance figures remain provisional until simulations/playtesting. Story choices may change relationships, dialogue, regional conditions, faction epilogues and cosmetics, but cannot strand characters or gate essential progression. Every phase has explicit acceptance criteria.
