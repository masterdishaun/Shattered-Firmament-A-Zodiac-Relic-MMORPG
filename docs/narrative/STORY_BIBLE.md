# Story Bible — Shattered Firmament

**Status:** Proposed canon 0.1

## Core premise

The Jade Firmament was a celestial engine and covenant regulating spiritual energy through twelve Zodiac Guardians. The Celestial Court centralised control and siphoned power from the Guardians while hiding the cost. A coalition tried to seize the engine during a rare alignment; astronomer Shen Veyu severed one central binding, believing the system could not be reformed. The engine fractured in the **Celestial Sundering**.

Twelve living seals scattered. Spiritual Karma flooded the world, corrupting forests, cities, shrines and beasts. Neon tombs appeared where no ruins had stood; some contain memories of futures that never happened. Mortals can no longer cultivate immortality as before, so Relic Hunters explore dangerous places to recover artefacts and stabilise the world.

**Central question:** If the power sustaining the world was built on a coercive covenant, should it be restored, destroyed or remade?

## Hidden truth

No single villain caused the Sundering. The Court drained the Guardians; rebels attempted a desperate seizure; Shen Veyu broke a binding. The Guardians had begun refusing the covenant because it had become coercion. The disaster was also an attempted liberation.

Corrupted Guardians are beings with agency: some suffer, some seek release, some defend their territory and some prey on the vulnerable. Killing every Guardian or extracting every shard is not the only answer.

The broken engine's abandoned command intelligence, **the Hollow Regent**, speaks in the voices of people erased from history. It wants to restore the old engine exactly as it was because it cannot imagine a world outside its original protocol.

## World concepts

- **Spiritual Karma:** residue of choices, promises, grief and spiritual force; not inherently good or evil.
- **Ashen-Jade:** celestial energy crystallised around corrupted Karma. Careless extraction stabilises equipment but damages the land's memory and vitality.
- **Relic Hunters:** explorers, investigators and salvagers who recover artefacts, rescue spirits, uncover history and make a living.
- **Zodiac Soul Gear:** equipment shaped by Guardian power. Visual evolution can be spectacular; competitive power remains bounded and never purchasable exclusively.

## Factions

### Sovereign Sect — Law

Motto: “A world without order is a world where the powerful devour the helpless.” Protectors and disciplined cultivators maintain barriers, roads and evacuation routes through pure-jade wards and flying-sword traditions. **Blind spot:** paternalism, censorship and treating obedience as peace. **Start:** White Jade Bastion, a fortified mountain city whose upper terraces glow while lower districts bear hidden costs.

### Ashen Syndicate — Chaos

Motto: “No throne owns the future.” Outcasts, workers, engineers and mercenaries oppose institutional monopoly on spiritual power. They fuse corrupted stardust with relic-tech and dark alchemy. **Blind spot:** exploitation and dangerous experiments justified as freedom. **Start:** Cinderwake, a neon salvage city around a fallen shard; extraction sustains the community and harms it simultaneously.

### Astral Concord — Balance

Motto: “Power must answer to the life it touches.” Shrine-keepers, mystics, healers and beast-binders seek to calm Guardians and build a consensual new covenant. **Blind spot:** indecision, romanticising suffering and delaying urgent action. **Start:** Luminous Veil, a forest-and-river sanctuary of floating shrines and spirit-beast paths threatened by memory storms.

Factions are ideological choices, not simple good/evil alignments. Each has principled motives, internal dissent and a real cost to its worldview.

## Principal cast

- **Marshal Ren Kaid:** Sovereign defender who sealed a district believing a breach would spread; hides that residents were never evacuated.
- **Mira Voss:** Syndicate engineer and worker organiser who wants communities to control technology but approved a harmful extraction method.
- **Elder Suyin:** Concord beast-binder who hears Guardian memories and fears the world cannot survive a full break from the old engine.
- **Tamsin Rook:** independent archivist who reads memory-echoes and exposes attempts to monopolise history.
- **Shen Veyu:** missing astronomer whose journals suggest their consciousness is trapped in the engine.
- **The Hollow Regent:** abandoned command intelligence trying to restore the coercive original system.
- **The Twelve Guardians:** twelve distinct entities with names, motives, histories and outcomes beyond simply serving as bosses.

## Main story arcs

1. **The Missing Point (1–20):** faction opening incidents reveal a scratched Zodiac sigil and evidence the official history is false.
2. **Three Accounts of a Disaster (21–40):** cross-faction travel uncovers Court extraction, rebel seizure and Shen Veyu's severing.
3. **The Zodiac Refuses (41–60):** Guardians show agency; forceful purification and indiscriminate extraction worsen corruption.
4. **War for the Firmament (61–80):** faction conflict escalates around engine nodes; the Hollow Regent and old engine's memory cost are discovered.
5. **A Covenant Unwritten (81–100):** the final raid resolves the launch crisis; the world changes and factions must reform.

The player is not a solo prophesied saviour. Choices influence settlement recovery, public history, NPC relationships and faction epilogues. Shared-world outcomes remain coherent.

## Narrative rules

- Three starts differ in region, cast, mentor, dungeon, side quests and interpretation of the same mystery.
- All factions eventually access shared world content and essential progression.
- Choices cause visible, bounded consequences without irreversibly stranding characters.
- Essential story is solo-friendly; groups deepen but do not exclusively gate the central truth.
- Guardians may be fought, calmed, negotiated with or released depending on context.
- Side quests tell human-scale stories about cosmic decisions.
- Story data is versioned, localisable and reviewed for continuity.
